IIM Indore AI & Cybersecurity — What It Is

The Executive Programme in AI & Cybersecurity for Organizations from IIM Indore is a 6-month certificate for working professionals targeting AI and cybersecurity at the organisational strategy level. Fee: approximately Rs 1,95,000 + GST. No coding required. Designed for IT leaders, risk managers, CISOs, and senior managers dealing with technology risk and AI governance.

Curriculum — What You Learn

  • AI foundations for organisations — ML, NLP, deep learning concepts without implementation
  • AI governance — bias, fairness, explainability, regulatory compliance (DPDP Act India)
  • Cybersecurity strategy — threat landscape, cyber risk management, board-level cyber decisions
  • Zero trust architecture — principles without technical implementation
  • AI + cybersecurity intersection — adversarial AI, AI-enabled cyber threats
  • Incident response frameworks — business continuity, resilience planning

Who Should Apply

  • CISOs and IT security managers targeting C-suite with IIM management credential
  • Risk managers and compliance officers handling cyber and AI risk
  • IT managers transitioning to technology leadership or CISO roles
  • Senior managers with technology oversight responsibilities
  • Professionals preparing for cybersecurity board advisory roles

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IIM Indore AI Cybersecurity programme good?

Yes — IIM Indore is NIRF #7 in Management. The AI & Cybersecurity programme gives IIM Indore alumni status with a 6-month commitment and no coding requirement. At Rs 1,95,000 for an IIM institutional credential in AI and cybersecurity, it is strong value for IT managers and security professionals.

Who is IIM Indore AI Cybersecurity programme for?

The programme is designed for working professionals in IT management, cybersecurity, risk, and compliance roles who want AI and cyber strategy frameworks with an IIM credential. It is not a technical programme — no coding or security tools are taught. The focus is organisational governance and strategy.

IIM Indore AI Cybersecurity or IIM Kozhikode Analytics — which to choose?

If your primary focus is cybersecurity risk, AI governance and tech risk management: IIM Indore. If your primary focus is business analytics, data-driven decisions and GenAI tools for productivity: IIM Kozhikode. Both have no coding requirement and similar fees. Choose based on your role — risk/security vs analytics/strategy.

Generative AI Certifications in India 2026 — The Full Landscape

Generative AI certifications in India span from free 6-hour Google badges to 8-month IIM programmes at ₹2.1L. The right choice depends on your goal: quick upskilling and a badge (free/low cost), demonstrated AI strategy capability for business leaders (IIM), or deep technical GenAI skills (IIT/technical courses). All serve different career purposes.

IIM GenAI Certification — Why It's Different

IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI is not a technical certification — it is a management programme that includes GenAI as one pillar. The value is IIM alumni status, strategic GenAI application frameworks, and analytics decision-making capability alongside the GenAI content. For professionals who need a GenAI credential that simultaneously builds management credibility — this is the only option.

Free GenAI Certifications Worth Getting

  • Google AI Essentials (Coursera) — free, 10 hrs, strong LinkedIn signal
  • IBM Generative AI Fundamentals (Coursera) — free, covers foundational GenAI concepts
  • Deeplearning.AI AI for Everyone — Andrew Ng, free, best non-technical AI overview

The Recommended Stacking Strategy

For working professionals: (1) Start with free certifications — Google AI Essentials + IBM GenAI Fundamentals — builds baseline literacy at zero cost. (2) Add Microsoft AI-900 if in Azure-heavy organisation. (3) Pursue IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI for institutional credential + management framework + IIM alumni status.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which GenAI certification is best for getting a job in India 2026?

For getting into AI roles: Google AI Essentials (free) + Python basics + portfolio projects beat certifications alone. For senior management roles: IIM Kozhikode Analytics & GenAI (₹2.1L) gives the management credibility + IIM brand needed for AI strategy roles. Technical roles: IIT Roorkee DS/AI (₹2.5L) or Deeplearning.AI specialisations.

Is Google AI Essentials certificate worth it?

Yes for the price (free). Google AI Essentials on Coursera gives a widely recognised LinkedIn badge, covers practical AI use in workplace tools, and signals AI literacy to employers. It is not a deep credential but is a strong free signal. Pair it with hands-on GenAI tool experience for best effect.

What is the difference between AI certification and AI course?

AI certification typically means a formal assessment with a credential issued after passing (Google, Microsoft, AWS AI certs). AI course means programme completion without a formal standardised exam (most IIM and IIT programmes, Coursera courses without separate exam). In practice, both add CV value — the institution's brand matters more than the format.
